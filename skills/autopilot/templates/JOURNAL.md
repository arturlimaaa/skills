# Journal

Append-only. One entry per iteration, newest at the bottom. Never rewrite or delete an entry,
including one that records a mistake. The record of a wrong turn is what stops the next iteration
repeating it.

Entry shape is fixed by `PROMPT.md` step 5.

**A journal that only records successes is a marketing document.** Write the deviations, the
defects found and not fixed, the gates that lied, and the checkpoints whose own wording was wrong.
Those entries are what the next iteration actually needs.
